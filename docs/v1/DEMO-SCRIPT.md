# ThriftOS — 5-minute demo for a Sher Shah owner

**Who it is for:** the owner (seth) of a landa business in Sher Shah — imports containers, has munshis sorting
in the godown, sells bales to traders from Karachi, Lahore and Peshawar, keeps udhaar in a register.

**What he should feel after 5 minutes:** "I can see which supplier makes me money, my munshi can use this without
training, and my udhaar is under control — on my phone."

**Before you go**

- Run `npm run demo:reset` the same morning (today's sales are seeded for today).
- On your phone, open the app once on Wi-Fi so it works offline later (see the release notes, "Demo on a phone").
- Sign in as the owner: `owner@almadina.demo` / `Demo@1234`. The app opens in Urdu; the switch is in **More**
  (مزید → English) if he prefers English.
- Battery full, brightness up, WhatsApp installed. Have a second phone or tab signed in as the munshi
  (Munshi tab: `0300 7860786`, PIN `1234`) if you want to show both.

Numbers below are from a fresh demo; they move a little as you use it. Say "around".

---

## 0:00 — The moment: which supplier makes money (60 s)

**Tap:** Home → the first card, **Profit per container** (فی کنٹینر منافع) → scroll to **By country** / **By supplier**.

**Say (English):**
"Seth sahib, this is your last six containers. Look here — American maal: about **Rs 30 profit on every kilo you
bought**, and **18% comes out A grade**. Korean maal is cheaper to buy, but only **10% A grade** — and it makes
**half the profit per kilo**, about Rs 14. Right now you know this from experience. Here it is in numbers, for every
container, the moment the bales are sold."

**Say (Roman Urdu):**
"Seth sahib, yeh aap ke pichhle chhe container hain. Yahan dekhein — American maal par **har kilo par taqreeban 30
rupay munafa**, aur **18% A grade** nikalta hai. Korean maal sasta milta hai, lekin sirf **10% A grade** — aur
munafa **aadha**, taqreeban 14 rupay kilo. Yeh baat aap tajurbe se jaante hain; yahan har container ka hisaab
number mein hai, jaise hi gaanthein bikti hain."

**Pause.** Let him read the list. He will compare with what he thinks. Ask: *"Is that what you see in your godown?"*

## 1:00 — Udhaar and today (40 s)

**Tap:** back to Home → **Udhaar owed** (باقی اُدھار) → the list opens with the biggest first, with days overdue.

**Say:** "Every customer, how much, and since how many days. Look at the days — Imran Landa Centre has not paid for a long time.
One tap: **Remind** sends him a WhatsApp in Urdu with the exact amount."
**Roman Urdu:** "Har gaahak, kitna baqi hai, kitne din se. Din dekhein — Imran Landa Centre ne kab se kuch nahi diya.
Ek tap: **Yaad dahani** — usay Urdu mein WhatsApp chala jata hai, poori raqam ke saath."

**Tap:** a customer → **Remind** → WhatsApp opens with the message (don't send).
Back to Home: **Today's sales** — "and this is what you sold today, live, even when you are not in the godown."
**Roman Urdu:** "Aur yeh aaj ki bikri hai — aap godown mein na bhi hon, phone par nazar aati hai."

## 1:40 — A container arrives (50 s)

**Tap:** **Maal aya** (مال آیا) → **Container** → supplier **Global Textile Recyclers** → **Invoice weight** 2000 →
**USD** → **Purchase cost** 1300 → **Dollar rate** 280 → chip **Duty** 48000 → chip **Clearing** 30000.

**Say:** "Look at the bottom: the landed cost per kilo — Rs 221 — before you even save. Duty, clearing, transport,
the dollar rate — all in. Save." → **Save container**.
**Roman Urdu:** "Neeche dekhein — ek kilo kitne ka pada, 221 rupay — save karne se pehle hi. Duty, clearing,
transport, dollar ka rate — sab shaamil. Save."

## 2:30 — The munshi sorts (60 s) — *the "no training" moment*

**Tap:** **Chhantai** (چھانٹی) on the saved screen → tap **Jeans** → keypad **900** → OK → **T-Shirts** 700 →
**Jackets** 250.

**Say:** "This is the munshi's screen. Big buttons, numbers only. Tap what he sorted, type the kilo, done —
five seconds. Wrong number? **Undo**. No internet in the godown? It saves on the phone and sends later."
**Roman Urdu:** "Yeh munshi ki screen hai. Bade button, sirf number. Jo chhanta us par tap, kilo likhein, ho gaya —
paanch second. Ghalat number? **Wapas lein**. Godown mein internet nahi? Phone mein mehfooz, baad mein chala jata hai."

**Tap:** **Close lot** → it asks once: "150 kg not sorted goes to waste" → close.
**Say:** "And immediately: how much came out of each category, and the cost per kilo of each."
**Roman Urdu:** "Aur foran: har qism kitni nikli, aur har ek ka kilo kitne ka pada."

## 3:30 — Bale, sticker, sale on udhaar, WhatsApp bill (60 s)

**Tap:** **Stock** (in More) → **Make bale** → **Jeans · A** → 50 kg → **Save bale** → **Print label**.
**Say:** "Every bale gets a QR sticker — scan it when it is sold, no mistakes about which bale went where."
**Roman Urdu:** "Har gaanth par QR sticker — bechte waqt scan, kaun si gaanth kahan gayi, koi ghalti nahi."

**Tap:** **Becho** (بیچو) → **New sale** → **Haji Rafiq & Sons** → **Scan bale** (or search the code) →
price 45000 → **Udhaar** → **Save sale** → **Share bill on WhatsApp**.
**Say:** "His last price comes up by itself. Udhaar, saved, and the bill goes to him on WhatsApp — he opens a link,
sees the bill. No paper."
**Roman Urdu:** "Us ka pichhla rate khud aa jata hai. Udhaar, save, aur bill WhatsApp par us ke paas — link kholta
hai, bill dekhta hai. Kaghaz nahi."

## 4:30 — Payment and back to Home (30 s)

**Tap:** **Khata** (کھاتہ) → Haji Rafiq → **Payment received** → 20000 → **JazzCash** → **Save payment**.
Then **Home**.

**Say:** "He paid twenty thousand on JazzCash — his khata is updated, and your home screen already shows it: udhaar,
today's sales, stock. That's your whole business, on your phone, in Urdu."
**Roman Urdu:** "Us ne bees hazaar JazzCash kiye — khata update, aur aap ki home screen par foran: udhaar, aaj ki
bikri, stock. Aap ka poora karobar, aap ke phone par, Urdu mein."

## Close (the ask)

"Give me one container. Your munshi enters it here for a month, I set it up with your categories in five minutes.
At the end of the month you tell me if you know your profit per supplier better than today."
**Roman Urdu:** "Mujhe ek container dein. Aap ka munshi ek mahina yahan entry kare, main aap ki qismon ke saath paanch
minute mein set kar deta hoon. Mahine ke aakhir mein aap batayein — kya aap ko har supplier ka munafa aaj se behtar
pata hai?"

### If he asks…

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Internet nahi hota godown mein." | Works offline; the badge on top says how many entries are waiting; they send themselves when the phone gets signal. |
| "Munshi ko kaun sikhaye ga?" | He signs in with his phone number and a 4-digit PIN; wrong PIN 5 times locks it, you unlock it from Settings → Team. |
| "Profit per kilo itna kam kyun?" | It is profit already made, over every kilo bought. Most of the container is not sold yet — the number grows as bales sell. Compare suppliers, not the size. |
| "Main sorting nahi karta, seedha bechta hoon." | Sign in as `owner@lahorebales.demo`: no sorting at all, ready stock, open a bale into pieces at 300 / 500 / 800. |
| "FBR invoice?" | Not in v1 (see the release notes). |
| "Mera data kahan hai?" | On your own server / account; each business sees only its own data. |
