- 2026-11-05 — 6.6 Sort, grade and bale in one step (from a vendor's feedback on the demo: his workers grade while they sort; a clothing gaari takes 3 days to a week with 3–8 bales a day; kat is not weighed and is sometimes sold):
  - **One screen** (Chhantai → lot): tiles are category + grade (e.g. CCR · 1, Baby · 2) when the business grades, else categories. Tap a tile → a sheet: **Bales** (how many, kg per bale — pre-filled from the category's usual bale weight or the last one used on this phone, editable) or **Loose kg** (the keypad opens at once). Bales go **straight into stock** with QR stickers ("Print stickers for the 3 bales of CCR · 2"); loose kg is in Make bale **at once**, before the lot is closed. The lot stays open for days; the screen shows today's bales and kg and the total. Undo takes an entry back while nothing from it is used (bales unsold), including part of loose kg.
  - **Closing the lot** (when the gaari is finished): what is left is **kat** — no weighing — booked as waste and put **into stock** as one loose-kg item ("Waste/Katran · LOT-…", source KAT) at **zero cost**, to sell by kg in a normal sale or **Throw away kat**. Kat is not counted in Home's "stock ready". Untouched loose kg of a category without a grade becomes one output at close (what the old separate grading step takes; that step is kept only for lots sorted before).

## Decisions
- Sorting (6.6): bales made while a lot is open are costed at the lot's cost per kg and re-costed at close; sold ones keep their sale cost (logged adjustment). Kat goes to stock at zero cost, so a kat sale is pure extra profit and bale costs are right whether or not kat sells.
- 6.2 Play Store app dropped by the owner (2026-11-05): the installable web app (PWA, "Add to Home screen" in Chrome) is how ThriftOS goes on phones; no Play Console account, store listing or Android package. Phase 6 is complete.
- Printing (6.3): everything is printed as a picture (raster), not printer text commands, so Urdu, QR codes and the layout look the same on every printer and in the PDF; the cost is a few KB more per bill over Bluetooth (about 2–4 seconds on slow printers).
- Import (6.1): stateless preview/commit (the file is sent twice) instead of storing uploads on the server; duplicates are stopped by `ImportedRow` row hashes, not by file hashes, so a re-saved or renamed file is still recognised.
- Where the work happens: tests, installs and the build run in the main folder (`/Users/zaigham/Data/me-projects/thriftos`). The agent's own session sits in a worktree it cannot leave and may not edit the main folder's files directly, so it edits and commits on a branch in its worktree, pushes to `origin/v2-go-live`, and fast-forwards `v2-go-live` in the main folder. Every commit and tag therefore ends up in the main folder too.
- Production runs Caddy in front of nginx (`web`) and the API: Caddy only does HTTPS, headers and routing; nginx keeps the cache rules from v1 4.2.
- Browser tests locally: `PW_DATABASE_URL` = `DATABASE_URL` with the database renamed to `thriftos_pw_test` (the default `thriftos:thriftos` user exists only in CI).
